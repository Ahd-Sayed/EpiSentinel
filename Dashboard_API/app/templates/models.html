⚔️ Models Analytical Strengths & Weaknesses

Model Strengths Weaknesses Metric Target
Isolation Forest Unsupervised anomaly detection. Provides the longest early-warning lead time (~35 days average). Sensitive to initial outlier cases. High false positive rate if utilized alone (F1 score of 0.123). Requires other models to filter noise. Lead Time
DBSCAN Clustering Spatial-temporal clustering. Excellent discriminative performance (ROC-AUC of 0.921, Precision of 0.489). Filters ambient noise. Density-based thresholds are not directly portable to narrow time series analysis windows due to data volume changes. ROC-AUC / FPR
LSTM Recurrent Network Deep learning recurrent forecasting. Models temporal sequence dependencies accurately. Achieves high F1 precision (0.644) for next-week forecast. Requires a substantial amount of positive outbreak training instances. Not available for 2 out of 12 low-signal scenarios. F1 Score
